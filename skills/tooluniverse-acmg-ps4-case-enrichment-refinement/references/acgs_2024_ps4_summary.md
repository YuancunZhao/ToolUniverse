# ACGS 2024 PS4 Case-Enrichment Summary

Source:

- ACGS Best Practice Guidelines for Variant Classification in Rare Disease 2024, v1.2.

## Key PS4 Points

- Formal case-control evidence should be preferred when available.
- Odds ratios, confidence intervals, and ancestry matching should be recorded.
- gnomAD can be used as a control population, but caution is needed when affected individuals may be included or when subpopulations are poorly represented.
- Rare-disease case-control data are often unavailable.
- In rare-disease practice, one unrelated affected individual with a rare and specific phenotype and population absence may support `PS4_Supporting`.
- Two or more unrelated affected individuals with rare and specific phenotype may support `PS4_Moderate`.
- For recessive disorders, rare biallelic cases should usually be counted through PM3 rather than PS4 when genotype/phase data are available.

## Guardrails

- Do not use PS4 from database pathogenicity assertions without case details.
- Do not double count the same proband as PS4 and PM3, PS2/PM6, or PP1.
- Do not count related family members as independent PS4 cases.
- Check duplicate publication/database reporting.
- Use VCEP case-count rules when available.
