# ACGS 2024 PM4/BP3 Summary

Source:

- ACGS Best Practice Guidelines for Variant Classification in Rare Disease 2024, v1.2.

## PM4

- Used for in-frame deletions/insertions in non-repeat regions and for stop-loss variants when the effect is protein extension rather than decay.
- Single amino-acid in-frame deletions/insertions should usually be supporting unless gene-specific evidence supports moderate.
- Conservation, region function, population frequency, and indel predictors can help determine applicability.
- PM4 can be used for last-exon truncating variants in genes where disease is caused by gain-of-function effects.
- PVS1 should be used for exon-scale deletions and duplications causing LoF.
- PM4 should not be applied if PVS1 is used for the same consequence.

## BP3

- Used for in-frame indels in repetitive regions without known function.
- Requires assessment of nucleotide/protein conservation, regional function, and population frequency.
- Do not apply BP3 if the repeat region is disease-relevant or part of a dominant-negative/gain-of-function mechanism.
